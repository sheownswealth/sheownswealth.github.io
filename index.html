Meet the Founder

Building businesses.
Creating opportunities.
Empowering women.

Malani is a serial entrepreneur, business builder, and passionate champion for women's empowerment. Her entrepreneurial journey has taught her that success isn't about waiting for the perfect opportunity. It's about having the confidence to create one.

Through building, learning, taking risks, and refusing to give up when things didn't go as planned, she discovered the power that comes from creating income on your own terms.

She created SheOwnsWealth to help other women recognize that same power within themselves. Her mission is to share real resources, business opportunities, and practical strategies that help women turn ideas into income and income into greater independence.

“When a woman owns her income, she owns more of her choices.”

SheOwnsWealth is more than a business. It's her way of reminding women that they are capable of building, creating, leading, and becoming the woman they've always pictured themselves being.
