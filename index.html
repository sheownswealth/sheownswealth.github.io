Meet the Founder

Meet Malani.
Entrepreneur. Business Builder. Champion for Women.

Malani is an entrepreneur, business builder, and passionate champion for women's empowerment. Her journey taught her that success isn't about waiting for the perfect opportunity. It's about having the confidence to create one.

Through building, learning, taking risks, and refusing to give up when things didn't go as planned, she discovered the power that comes from creating income on your own terms.

She created She Owns Wealth to help other women recognize that same power within themselves. Her mission is to share real resources, business opportunities, and practical strategies that help women turn ideas into income and income into greater independence.

“When a woman owns her income, she owns more of her choices.”

She Owns Wealth is more than a business. It's a reminder that women are capable of building, creating, leading, and becoming the women they've always pictured themselves being.
